

# Anakrid
:

## Background
The Anakrid is a mutated ant like creature, sporting high dexterity in exchange for reduced consitution.






## Features
**Ability Score Increase:**                   :: Two different ability scores of your choice increase by 2 and 1 respectivily.
:
**Age:**     :: Anakrid's have relatvily short life spans. They reach maturity around age 5 and die at 45 - 55 years
:
**Alignment:** :: Anarkid's a generally docile creatures, unless their way of life is threatened. Once threatnened the Anakrid is quick to act and dispatch enemies.
:
**Size:**                   :: Medium
:
**Speed**     :: You have a speed of 45
:
**Languages:** :: You can read speak and write common, and Arician.
:
**Agile Build:** :: You have a base +5 to dexterity, and your dexterity can reach a maximum of 25.
:
**Fragile Frame:** ::  You cannot raise your constitution above 9 inclduing magical items.
