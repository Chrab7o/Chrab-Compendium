# Golemancer
## DISCLAIMER 
Hi unlike Oracle I have no idea how balanced I think this class will be. I encourage you to try it, but I might have to make changes mid campaign to make it more balanced.
## Background
The Golemancer has the power to speak life into organic objects. Golemancers are one with nature similar to druids, but instead of their magic manifesting in pure magical prowess they prefer to imbue their magic into the world and make new life.

## Playstyle
The Golemancer specializes in making golems from earth and clay. At the start of the day the Golemancer will make a number of golems allowed to the amount of Breath Of Life they possess. The more complex the golem is the more Breath of Life it will require to bring that Golem to the world of the living. The Golemancer can then unleash those golems to do tasks or fight on the Golemancer's behalf, until dismissed or destroyed.

## Alignment
Golemancers are often good natured people. Many do not use the golems to fight but rather as helpers for smaller villages. Those who use golems to fight often do so for noble causes such as defense of their homes and loved ones. Some evil aligned golemaners do exist however. Their evil disposition is present in the way the golems manifest themselves by being more cold and stoic, when  compared to the more kind and loving good natured golems.

## Creating an Golemancer
When creating a Golemancer consider where you want your character to come from. Many Golemancers grow up around druids, or nature, and come from areas where organic objects are in abundance. Consider whether your character chooses the life of adventure or was forced into it by an external circumstance. Lastly, determine what your golemancers ultimate goal is at the journey's end.

### Level 1 Mold Golems
At the end of each long rest you spend some time forming Golem cores. Each Golem core represents a respective archetype. You can make a number for cores equal to your core limit. Your cores level up alongside you becoming stronger as you do.

### Level 1 Summon Golem
As an action mold some clay around a Golem core and summon a golem. The golem acts on your initiative and communicates in Common. You can instruct it to use movement, action, and bonus action etc where applicable. Your Golem can receive both buffs and debuffs provided they do not alter the golems form (no polymorph,enlarge reduce etc)  You can only have one active golem at a time at this level. You can use an action to dismiss a golem if it is within 5 ft of you. Your golem must stay within 500 ft of you or else they will deconstruct.

##### Level 3 Steadfast Companion.
This golem is unique in that it stays with you at all times, and does not count against your standard golem summoning limit. When this Golem is destroyed you can rebuild him an hour later once the core has recharged. This Golem starts with 12 in all stats, a speed of 25, a plus 2 proficiency, and gains a flat 6 hp every level up +  its con. At every level you may put two points into the stat of your choice including this one. The Golem is proficient  in every weapon and armor type. Your golem follows the standard action, bonus action, action economy. Your Golem starts with 2 features of level 3 or lower from any class. You will gain more as you level up. You can feed the golem a spell scroll to teach it a spell. It can only learn up to level 2 spells at level 3 and it can only consume one spell per day. All spell levels have their own cool down, and you cannot cast ANY spells while that cool down is in effect. Level 1 and 2 spells have a 1 turn cool down after being cast. You will be assigned a cool down status that will denote how many turns until you can cast a spell of that level again..
