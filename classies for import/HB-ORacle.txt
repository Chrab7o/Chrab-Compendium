# The Oracle


## Background
The oracle specialises in both predicting the future, they also aim to tip the scales in their favor. Oracle's main mechanics revolve around Manifestations and Portents. Portents function similarly to Divination Wizard Portent Dice, and Manifestations are predictions made on creatures that if fate agrees will make powerful effects come true. 

## Playstyle
The Oracle emphasizes utility and high variance damage. You will either feel very weak or very useful, not for a player that values consistency. 

## Alignment
Oracles can be of any alignment, but most Oracles of both good and evil persuasions hold themselves in high regard to a sense of principles.

## Creating an Oracle
When creating an Oracle consider what stage of power you want to be in. Within the world of Alva anyone can be born with the gift of foresight. You can play someone who just became aware of their powers, or a person of high social standing who has been cast or moved from their post for a given reason.
}}

### Level 1 Manifestations
As an oracle you can manifest blessings on your allies. As a Bonus Action, you can bless another creature within 60 feet of yourself who can see or hear you. That creature gains one of your Manifestation dice. A creature can have only one Manifestation die at a time. Your Manifestation die is a d10 becomes a d8 at level 5 , a d6 at level 10 and a d4 at level 15. 

Once within the next hour when the creature heals, or does damage, the creature can pick 3 possible results: roll the Manifestation die if the die lands on one of the 3 chosen results and add an extra 2d4 to your total. If the creature misses the predicted roll add 1d4 instead. 

Number of Uses. You can confer a Manifestation die a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

At Higher Levels. When your Manifestation die levels up your bonus effect die goes up with it. d6 at level 5, d8 at level 10 and d10 at level 15. All manifestations require vocal components


### Level 2 Portent Rolls
At the end of a Long rest roll a number of d20 in accordance to the portent dice table. You can replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by you or a creature that you can see with one of these foretelling rolls. You must choose to do so before the roll, and you can replace a roll in this way only once per turn.

Each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a long rest, you lose any unused foretelling rolls. 




### Level 2 Predictive Sight
As an oracle you have learned to anticipate threats right before they strike. 
As an action, empower your sight. For the next minute enemies cannot get advantage on you. In a situation where a creature would get advantage and disadvantage agaisnt you it does not cancel out, and the creature would just have disadvantage.


### Level 3 Oracle Path
All Oracles choose a path to hone their skills for a specific purpose
Pick one of the following subclasses. You will gain new Oracle Path features at levels 6, 10, and 14


#### Foresight Spellblade
Foresight Spellblades are oracles who prefer to take on a more damage dealing playstyle. 

##### Level 3 Spellblade training
You gain proficiency in Simple weapons, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords, light and medium armor.



##### Level 3 Preemptive strikes
You are able to predict the way an enemy moves when you strike them allowing you to add your wisdom modifier to attack and damage rolls instead of str / dex 
\page
#### Tactician 
The Tactician fully embraces the support playstyle choosing to support the battlefield by analysing all possible outcomes.

##### Level 3 Analyze
Analyze your targets to get an upper hand. As an action pick a number of creatures within 30 feet of you equal to your wisdom modifier that you can see. Pick one of the following pieces of information to learn from all of them. AC, HP, Number of weaknesses, Number of Resistances, speed. Pick a value and the dm will tell you if the correct value is higher, lower, or exact. For HP you can ask in percentages. You pick one number for all selected creatures. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus 

#### Portent Divinator
The Portent Divinator focuses on using their portent abilities to the fullest.

##### Extra Portents
Gain an extra portent dice

##### Portent Revolution
When you receive your portent dice each day, you can reroll. You must use the new roll

You have learned to convert the magical energy required for probability to create randomization.

All blessings and curses require verbal and somatic components. Creatures can only have one blessing or curse at the same time. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your wisdom modifier
