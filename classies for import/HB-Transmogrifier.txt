# Transmogrifier
:

## Background
The Transmogrifier is a person who through unatural or magical means has gained the ability to morph their bodies into different forms. 

## Playstyle
The Transmogrifier class revolves around a short range attack pattern with multiple movement options.



## Creating a Transmogrifier
When creating a Mutant consider the origion you want for your character. Has your character gained their power by choice? Did they make a deal with a powerful creature, or were they cursed. Then consider how your charcter feels about their abilities. Does your character see them as a curse or a boon.

### Level 1 Transmogrifier Origin
At level 1 you have become a Transmogrifier and must select an origin of your abilities. Your origin will determine the asthetic of your transformations. 
#### Abyssal Origin
With the Abyssal Origin your features will manifest in eldrich forms such as tendrils.

#### Fey Origin
With the gey Origin your features will manifest as beastial features.

#### Undead Origin
With the Undead Origin your features will manifest as skeletal alterations and extensions

#### Monsterous Origin
With the Monsterous Origin your features will manifest as fleshy mutations

### Level 1 Organic Weapon
A need to survive has taught you to create natural weapons by altering your arms or legs.

As an action transform an appendge into a weaponized form. This weapon counts as a natrual weapon for you, cannot be removed via disarming features, and can appear however you like. However your Organic Weapon must functionally have one of the following forms.

As you level up you will unlock better weapon forms.

### Level 1 Unarmored Defense
When not wearing armor gain an ac equal to 10 + dex + con








### Level 2 Grapple Claw
As you get used to your new body you find it difficult to deal with ranged attackers that you cannot reach. In an effort to close the gap you figure out how to great a grapple claw out of your arms. As a bonus action pick a creature within 30ft that you can see and launch a claw at them. The grappled target can make a strength saving throw to break the claw. The Dc for this check is equal to 8+ profiency + your strength modifier. On a failure you are pulled within 5ft of the target. There must be atleast 5ft of open space between you and the target otherwise you will be stopped. Effects that alter your movement on the field will still push or pull you as you move past them. Hitting a wall will result in you taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage.

### Level 4 Ability Score Increase
You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Necromancer levels 8, 12, and 16



### Level 5 Multi Attack
At level 5 you gain the ability to make a second attack when using the attack action on your turn.

### Level 5 Detatch
Remove one a part of your body to control it remotley. If the detached body part can transform into a weapon you can attack using it. Detached body parts are extensions of you and use your own action economy. If you are without legs and use your hands to move you can only use half your movement. If you are without and arms or legs, and must resort to hoping you have a movement of 5ft.  For the purpose of damage any detached body parts can be attacked, but area of effect attacks will not damage you multiple times.


















### Level 6 Adaptive Movement
You have learned to alter the shape of your feet to move more freely. While not wearing footwear you are not effected by difficult terrain. 
\page

## Level 7 Amorphis Mouth
You have learned to Mimic the voices of the people around you. When someone whos face you can see speaks in a language you dont understand you can morph your mouth to translate your speach into the mimiced language. You do not learn the language outright and cannot understand what is being said to you.



 



















### Level 8 Ability Score Increase
You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Necromancer levels 8, 12, and 16, 18


### Level 9 Alter Self
Though hours of practice you learn to not only morph your body for a utilitarian purpose, but to morph the appearce aswell. As an action you can change the appearance of your body to appear as someone else it takes a mintue to complete the transformation. You do not adopt the clothing of the creature you imitate, and changing into a creature that would not support your clothing will cause your clothing/ armor to break.



### Level 10 Unwavering Heart
As your grasp over Transmogrification grows, your heart has had to evovle to keep up. As an action overcharge your heart granting you hp per turn equal to your profiency bonus. This effect lasts for a minute. This effect recharges on a long rest.
