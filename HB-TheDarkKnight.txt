# The Dark Knight


## Background
The Dark Knight embraces their inner darkness to let their hatred fuel them with power.

## Alignment
Dark Knight's aren’t necessarily evil. However as the darkness unleashed grows their grasp of moraility dimishes.

## Creating a Dark Knight
When creating a Dark Knight think about why your character might want to embrace their inner darkness. Dark Knights are often those hurt by tragedy, have debilitating diseases, terminal illness, or are simply too weak to accomplish their desires on their own. In the world of Alva anyone can become a Dark Knight without much Cultivation, but the cost of poisoning one's soul often deters most from taking the plunge  
}}

## Class Features

As a notary, you gain the following class features

#### Hit Points
**Hit Dice:**                    :: 1d10 per notary level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:**     :: 10 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** :: 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per notary level after 1st

#### Proficiencies
**Armor:**   :: Medium armor, Light armor, Heavy armor
**Weapons:** :: Martial, Simple, Shield
**Tools:**   :: None

**Saving Throws:** :: Strength, Constitution
**Skills:**        :: Choose two from Insight, Deception, Intimidation, Athletics



#### Equipment
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:
-   (a) a quarterstaff or (b) a dagger
-   (a) a component pouch or (b) an arcane focus
-   (a) a scholar's pack
-   A Glass Orb

{{text-align:center

**Spell attack modifier**:: = your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier

**Dark Arts die**:: = 1d8

### Level 3 Dark Knight Archetype
Dark Knights often subscribe to one of 3 stances that affect their attack style. Pick one of the following stance styles. 


#### Vanguard of Darkness
A defense orientated archetype. 
Vanguard Stance, as a bonus action focus your energies on reducing damage. For the next minute on the start of your turn gain 1d10 temp hp, this can stack but cannot exceed your current max hp. For the duration your attacks have disadvantage as you focus your energy on maintaining the barrier. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your profiency bonus.



#### Harbinger of Darkness
A melee offensive orientated archetype. 
**Death stance**, as a bonus action let the darkness consume you to enter a death stance for one minute. In this state you gain advantage on attack rolls. On hit you may roll 1d10 and deal that much in additional necrotic damage, but you take that amount of damage as well. On a crit restore 1d10 + con in hp. The first time you would go down in this state you may expend a hit die if you have any to get back up with that much hp. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your profiency bonus. You do not lose Death Stance, on revive if you use a hit dice.




#### Magus of Darkness
A caster orientated archetype. You gain the ability to cast the following spells as Dark Arts using Darkness. Each spell has a cost of its base level ( level 1 spell costs 1 darkness) you can spend an addtional Darkness to upcast the spell. On taking this subclass gain a new Dark Art Shadow Bolt

#### Shadow Bolt
As an action consume 0 darkness to unleash a bolt of darkness that deals 1d8 + con necrotic damage on hit. You attack roll benifits from your spell attack modifier

### Level 5 Mark of Darkness
When you touch or hit a creature you can channel your dark energy to mark them. You can track the marked creature through walls and magical darkness up to 1000 ft away. You can only have one creature marked at a time. If you kill a marked creature you can reapply this mark on a new creature for free. Otherwise, you can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus a day. Recharge on a long rest.

### Level 9 Imposing Presence
You emanate an aura of fear and evil. You gain advantage on intimidation checks made on creatures within 20 ft of you. As a bonus action spread darkness around you in a radius of 20 ft. ### Level 10 Soul Bound
You have learned that you can bind your soul to objects and move between them. You can now attune yourself to a total of 4 objects. You can teleport those 4 items to you or teleport yourself to those items. You must spend one hour attuning to each object, and must unattuned or destroy the object before you can attune to a new one. Objects can be no larger than 8 ft, and weigh more than 80 pounds. You can teleport the items to yourself at will, but can only teleport yourself to one of your items once per long rest. Additionally, all soul bound weapons now count as magical for damage purposes.
